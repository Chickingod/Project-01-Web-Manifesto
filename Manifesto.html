Ramona's Internet Manifesto
Manifesto

By Ramona

One of the greatest yet most detrimental things to be a part of the internet is the concept of Artificial Intelligence. Whether it is the navigation system in your gps, or the chat bot that some people develop feelings for, it is all around us.

To me it is simple:

As long as it is not used for one's self gain while putting others below, I accept this use.

If it is used to hurt others or to only benefit oneself, I dislike that use and I feel it should be prohibited.
